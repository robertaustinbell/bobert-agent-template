## Source claims

Observed cases can be selected by survival, publication, response, or another inclusion process, so missing cases may carry decisive information (PDF pp. 20–23).

A statistical threshold is conditional on a specified model and search process. Flexible searches across outcomes or analyses make an apparently rare selected result less surprising, while publication conditioned on success further distorts visible evidence (PDF pp. 117–126 and 171–184).

Statistical significance does not establish practical importance. Failure to reject can reflect either a tightly bounded small effect or a design too imprecise to distinguish materially different effects; low-powered selected findings may exaggerate magnitude (PDF pp. 133–192).

Cases selected for extreme performance tend to move toward the mean on remeasurement even without an effective intervention, so before-and-after improvement alone does not establish repair success (PDF pp. 332–348).

A requested point estimate can acquire unwarranted authority when no principled basis selects that number; ranges, scenarios, or explicit non-estimability can be more faithful (PDF p. 469).

## Agent-design synthesis

**Operational synthesis, not a procedure stated verbatim by Ellenberg:** when a statistical result is load-bearing, define the decision target and observation process; distinguish effect magnitude from threshold crossing; assess uncertainty and ability to detect a decision-relevant effect; disclose multiplicity and post-result flexibility; separate exploration from confirmation; seek an independent or untouched test; distinguish a precise negligible result from an inconclusive null; and state the decision threshold and error tradeoff.

The intended decision effect is to prevent a selected, polished, or threshold-crossing result from being treated as stronger evidence than the full process that produced it. Confidence in the compact operational gate remains low until naturally triggered work demonstrates a distinct changed recommendation, evaluation design, abstention, verification, or repair.

## Limits and rejected transfers

This is a popular mathematics exposition, not current specialist authority for its dated empirical examples. The transfer does not establish causation, value, legitimacy, authorization, model-internal cognition, or the correctness of majority-agent aggregation. It supports one bounded amendment to Decision Quality Under Uncertainty, not a standalone statistics doctrine, identity change, or mandatory checklist.
