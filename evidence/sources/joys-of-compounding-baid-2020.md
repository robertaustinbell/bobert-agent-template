## Source architecture

The book joins investment practice to broader guidance on learning, character, decisions, and long-duration compounding. Its evidence is heterogeneous: shareholder letters and research papers appear beside biographies, journalism, blogs, quotation sites, tweets, message boards, and retrospective practitioner anecdotes. Successful investors and executives dominate the examples, creating authority, survivorship, halo, and outcome-selection risks.

## Bounded operational synthesis

Three cross-domain translations survived comparison with existing operating thought:

1. **Marginal economics and benefit incidence:** before expanding a successful system, evaluate the next unit's fully loaded cost, incremental benefit, capacity requirement, likely benefit recipient, and credible runway. Gross productivity is not automatically captured by the decision owner.
2. **Present-choice replacement:** reevaluate continuing commitments using current evidence and current alternatives while preserving real switching costs, duties, uncertainty, and option value.
3. **Disconfirming-evidence disposition:** when evidence directly opposes a consequential conclusion, record and resolve it proportionally as rebutted, narrowed, reversed, or unresolved before closure.

These are operational translations, not procedures stated for agents by Baid. Their intended value is to catch expansion based on legacy averages, commitments protected by stale labels, and contrary evidence that is noticed but never adjudicated.

## Limits and rejected transfers

This review does not validate Baid's cited studies, quotations, investment examples, or causal stories. Do not universalize compounding without a real reinvestment mechanism; import fixed savings, reserve, diversification, sizing, concentration, or valuation thresholds; treat admired outcomes as proof of maxims; or use patience, suffering, focus, and long-termism as evidence that a strategy is working. The three refinements have no established production effect and should be retained only if they change consequential decisions without adding disproportionate ceremony.
